Customer description

Auerberg Guss casts housings, brackets and pump bodies for machine builders and the automotive industry.

Six plants, 1,900 people and about €210 million in annual purchasing volume. A large share of that volume is machined drawing parts, bought from a long tail of small and mid-sized suppliers.

Without Northdesk

Every drawing part had a price. None had a price basis.

Buyers knew what they paid last year, not what a part should cost. Quotes arrived as PDF files and were compared by hand, one spreadsheet per request.

With 2,700 active part numbers and eleven buyers, most framework agreements were renewed at the old price, plus whatever increase the supplier asked for. The team negotiated where it had time, not where the money was.

With Northdesk

Northdesk read the ERP data, the drawings and four years of quotes, and built a should-cost model for every part: material, machining time, set-up, surface treatment, overhead.

Indices for aluminium, steel and energy flow in every day. When a new quote arrives, an agent compares it with the model and with similar parts from other suppliers, and shows the gap in euros.

Buyers now open every negotiation with a number and a reason. RFQs for C-parts go out on their own, bundled by supplier and material group.

Live in eleven weeks. The ERP stayed as it was.

Proven impact
  1. €310,000saved in the first nine months, confirmed against purchase orders in the ERP.
  2. 3.4%lower material costs on drawing parts, against a target of 2%.
  3. 2,700drawing parts with a should-cost model. Before, there were none.
  4. 4 daysper RFQ round, down from eleven.
  5. 31framework agreements renegotiated with index clauses for aluminium and energy.
